# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Vite + React + TypeScript, chosen for a responsive single-page planner with client-side itinerary interactions and straightforward GitHub Pages deployment.

## Users

Friends visiting Seattle for one memorable day, planning a flexible coffee and attraction route.

## Product Purpose

Seattle Day Planner helps visitors turn a short list of Seattle coffee shops and attractions into a realistic one-day itinerary. Success means a visitor can quickly understand the route, reorder or add stops, and see the day represented geographically without needing a separate planning tool.

## Positioning

The planner pairs a visually rich Seattle guide with an editable, map-oriented day plan so a group can move from inspiration to a usable route in one sitting.

## Operating Context

Visitors use the planner before or during a day in Seattle, most likely on a laptop or phone while deciding what to do next. The primary flow is browsing places, adding them to the day, reordering the timeline, and checking the route on a map.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Show coffee shops and attractions with photos, categories, descriptions, and Seattle neighborhood context.
- Let visitors add and remove stops from a single-day itinerary and change stop order.
- Show the selected plan on a map-like route view with labeled stops.
- Keep the experience client-side and deployable as a static site on GitHub Pages.
- Use the provided image assets; do not fabricate testimonials, ratings, prices, opening hours, or other unsupported claims.
- The initial content set is intentionally curated from the supplied Seattle images and can be expanded later.

## Brand Commitments

- Working product name: Seattle Day Planner.
- The voice should feel like a thoughtful friend who knows Seattle well: warm, direct, and specific.
- The supplied Seattle photography is the primary visual evidence and should remain recognizable in the experience.

## Evidence on Hand

Provided assets in `assets\`: Caffe Ladro, Caffe Vita, Chihuly Garden and Glass, Espresso Vivace, Fremont Troll, Kerry Park, Monorail Espresso, Pike Place Market, Seattle Great Wheel, Space Needle, Starbucks Reserve, and Victrola.

No external ratings, venue schedules, map API credentials, testimonials, or brand guidelines were provided.

## Product Principles

- Make the next good decision obvious.
- Balance inspiration with a route that can actually fit into one day.
- Keep the map useful without making the interface feel like a logistics dashboard.
- Treat the supplied photography as the source of place and personality.
- Never hide uncertainty behind invented facts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic landmarks, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, readable contrast, alt text for venue imagery, and responsive layouts that remain usable at narrow widths.
